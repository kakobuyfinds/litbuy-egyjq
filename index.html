The quick answer

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

In shopping searches, a Litbuy spreadsheet generally means an organized list of product references for comparing finds. A useful list records the item link, category, size, materials, price, and the date checked. This independent guide explains that workflow; it is not an official Litbuy service or a live inventory feed.

Your comparison framework

Build a useful Litbuy product list.

Use these fields in your own spreadsheet. Clear records are more helpful than an oversized collection of unverified links.

Field to save What to record Why it matters
Product identity Exact name, category, product URL, and seller reference. Keeps similar-looking items from being confused.
Size & fit Selected size, unit of measurement, and item-specific dimensions. Makes comparisons more useful than size labels alone.
Materials & finish Stated composition, construction details, and close-up photo notes. Shows what is documented and what still needs checking.
Cost & currency Item price, currency, shipping estimate, fees, and tax estimate. Helps compare the expected total instead of only the headline price.
Terms & freshness Return conditions, availability notes, and the date you checked. Flags details that may change before purchase.

Before you browse

Clear answers.
Fewer assumptions.

A practical guide should make its scope as clear as its recommendations.

Is this an official Litbuy website?

No. This is an independent editorial guide. Browsing links lead to CAFANS, and this page does not provide a Litbuy account, checkout, or official support.

How should I compare spreadsheet finds?

Compare the exact item, selected size, measurements, stated materials, seller information, return terms, and total estimated cost. Check the live listing before paying.

Do the brand images prove authenticity or availability?

No. Brand images are editorial references. They do not establish authenticity, stock, seller authorization, or availability on CAFANS.

Does the budget planner show a shipping quote?

No. The planner adds your own estimates for items, shipping, fees, and taxes. It does not retrieve carrier prices or calculate destination-specific charges.

Can I download a spreadsheet here?

This page provides a comparison framework rather than a downloadable spreadsheet or live product database. Use the suggested fields to build your own list.

What should I check for clothing sizes?

Compare item measurements with a garment that already fits you. Check whether measurements describe the body or the garment, whether they are flat or full circumference, and whether units are centimeters or inches.
